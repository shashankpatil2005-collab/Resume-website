About Me

The Intersection of Engineering & Entrepreneurship

Growing up in a family of engineers, my childhood was immersed in discussions on schematics, mechanics, and technical problem-solving. This environment naturally wired my brain for rigorous engineering, analytical deduction, and systems design.

Yet, as I grew deeper into engineering, I realized that technical ingenuity alone isn't enough—the world changes when breakthrough technology is packaged into viable, scalable business models. My heart was always set on entrepreneurship, product leadership, and enterprise building.

To realize this vision, I chose to concurrently pursue an ECE Engineering degree alongside the BBA in Digital Business and Entrepreneurship at IIM Bangalore. This dual foundation enables me to speak the language of engineers while driving the strategy, economics, and user experience required to win in modern digital markets.
